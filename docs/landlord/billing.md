# Landlord billing

## Planned (Phase C, merchant billing)

Not built yet. The platform has plans and subscription status records, but
the landlord Billing area has no workflow for charging merchants for those
subscriptions, issuing subscription invoices, or reconciling subscription
payments. Paystack is to bill African merchants; whether Stripe Billing is
used for merchants elsewhere is still an open decision.

Do not confuse this future platform billing area with tenant
[Payments](../tenant/payments.md), which records shopper payments and refunds
for orders sold by a merchant's store. Plan entitlements are described in
[Plans and subscriptions](plans-and-subscriptions.md).
